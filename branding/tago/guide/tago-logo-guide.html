Logo guide

Tago (formerly PassVault). One page, for anyone putting the logo on something.

1. The logo

Idea: a T traced on Android's nine-dot unlock grid. The secret pattern only you know becomes the app's initial; the coral dot is where the trace starts. Tago is Tagalog for "hidden, kept out of sight".

Horizontal (primary) · stacked · symbol · reversed. Use the symbol alone only where the name is already present (app icon, favicon, avatar).

2. Clear space

Keep a clear zone of one node on every side: the diameter of one of the large dots in the T. It scales with the logo, so measure it on the artwork at the size you use.

Approved pairs: colour logo on paper or white · paper logo on ink · white logo on indigo · black or white one-colour anywhere else. CMYK values are converted, not proofed: proof with your printer, and ask them to match a Pantone from a bridge guide if you need a spot colour.

5. Typography

The wordmark is custom drawn: always use the files, never type "tago" in a font. In the app and in text, use the system font (Roboto on Android; sans-serif-medium for headings).

6. App icon

One colour: paper T on indigo. No coral on the launcher icon: at that size it reads as a notification dot. Android 13+ themed icons use the monochrome layer.

7. Don'ts

  • Don't stretch, squash, rotate or mirror the logo, or redraw the T as another letter.
  • Don't recolour outside the palette, or put coral anywhere but the start node.
  • Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or glows.
  • Don't rearrange or resize the symbol and wordmark within a lockup.
  • Don't drop the unused dots: without them it is just a T.
  • Don't place it on busy photos without a solid indigo or ink tile behind it.
