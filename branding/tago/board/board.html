The idea

The secret only you know, as the initial.

Every Android user knows the nine-dot unlock grid. Tracing a T on it turns the most personal secret on the phone, your pattern, into the app's first letter. The coral dot is where the trace begins. Tago is Tagalog for hidden, kept out of sight: two syllables, said TAH-go.

1 · Nine dots

2 · Trace 1-2-3-5-8

3 · The mark

The trace becomes a T. The unused dots keep the grid readable.

Colour

Indigo, with one warm spark.

Indigo is the app's own accent, and rare among password managers, which lean on red, blue and green. Coral marks the start of the trace and the app's main action; it is used small, never as a background.
